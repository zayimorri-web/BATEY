How it works

1. You book

Tell us the property, the checkout and the check-in time. Recurring hosts sync once and we watch the calendar with you.

2. We turn it over

Arrival photos first (your protection), then the full checklist: kitchen, baths, beds, floors, high-touch surfaces.

3. You see the proof

Photo report lands in your messages before check-in. Notes on damage, restock, or forgotten items included.

4. Guests review the clean

Cleanliness is the first thing guests rate. We keep yours at five stars.

Questions hosts ask

Do you bring linens? We change and stage the linens you provide; off-site laundering of your sets is available as an extra.

What if a guest damages something? We photograph it on arrival — before cleaning — and send it to you immediately, so your claim has evidence with a timestamp.

How do I pay? Through Turno if we work there, or 50% deposit by card before the job and the balance when the photo report is delivered.

What's your service area? Worcester plus 25 miles. Greater Boston quoted case by case.
